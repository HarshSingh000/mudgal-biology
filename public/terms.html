All course prices, entitlements, and mock test scores are calculated authoritatively on our servers. Any attempt to tamper with client state or bypass entitlement checks may result in account suspension.

Aspirants must keep community discussions respectful and exam-focused. Moderators may hide or remove reported content and mute or suspend accounts violating platform guidelines.
