🛡️ Mission-Mode Execution

STF — Selection Task Force

STF (Selection Task Force) is Mudgal Biology's disciplined, target-driven execution squad built to convert preparation into final selection.

  • Daily Micro-Targets: Fixed daily NCERT & Graduation Biology chapters with zero syllabus backlog.
  • RPSC & NEET PYQ Drills: High-yield previous year questions and negative-marked practice tests inside Student App.
  • Active-Recall Checkpoints: Flashcards, short-trick revision sheets, and weekly accuracy tracking.
  • Direct Accountability: Regular strategy check-ins, doubt resolution, and rank-improvement guidance.
