Refund & Cancellation Policy

Clear, transparent terms for course purchases and subscription passes on Mudgal Biology.

If your account was debited twice or an order failed to unlock access, contact Support with your Order ID. Verified duplicate charges are refunded to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.

3. Course & Pass Refunds

Digital course purchases and activated subscription passes are generally non-refundable once paid content or test series have been accessed, except in cases of verified technical non-delivery.
