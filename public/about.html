About Mudgal Biology

Empowering NEET Biology, Medical, and Government Exam aspirants with structured, affordable, high-retention digital education.

Our Mission

Mudgal Biology was built to make high-quality NCERT line-by-line Biology coaching and competitive exam preparation accessible on every student's phone and browser — without expensive coaching fees.

What We Offer

  • Live interactive marathon classes & recorded HD lectures with speed controls.
  • Smart E-Books, highlighted PDFs & active-recall Biology flashcards.
  • Server-timed, negative-marked mock tests with detailed explanations.
  • Dedicated faculty doubt desk & moderated aspirant community.

Academic Leadership & Portals

Led by Dr. Mudgal (Biology Lead) and subject specialists across Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning, Indian Polity, and General Studies. Students learn inside the Student App, while Faculty, Support Agents, and Administrators manage content and moderation securely inside the Admin Portal.
