About Mudgal Biology

Learn Biology the Smart Way — “Har topic ko simple banake padhate hain”. Founded by Hemant Mudgal (RPSC 2nd Grade Science 2022 Qualified, Senior Teacher).

Our Vision & Story

Started by Hemant Mudgal — RPSC 2nd Grade Science Exam 2022 Qualified and currently serving as a Senior Teacher (Govt. of Rajasthan) — Mudgal Biology reaches aspirants across India to deliver Biology education that is simple, relevant, and result-driven.

Target Exams & Offerings

  • RPSC 1st Grade Biology & RPSC 2nd Grade Science (Graduation Biology Series + GEMS Mentorship).
  • URATPG (Biology) & NEET (Bio Focused) with NCERT & B.Sc. level clarity.
  • Topic-wise lectures with short tricks, PYQ solutions, smart short-note e-books & flashcards.
  • Negative-marked online test series & dedicated doubt resolution inside the Student App.

Official Community & Portals

Watch free lectures on YouTube (@MUDGALBIOLOGY), join daily notes updates on Telegram (t.me/MudgalBiologyT), and access full batches, tests, and smart e-books inside the Student App.
