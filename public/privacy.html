Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • How Mudgal Biology collects, secures, and respects your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information required to deliver your courses and test series: your mobile phone number (for OTP authentication), name, optional email/state, target exam preference, lesson progress, mock test attempts, doubt tickets, and order history.

2. Payment Security

All payments are processed through verified Indian payment gateway partners using server-signed orders and HMAC-verified webhooks. Mudgal Biology never stores card numbers, CVV, or UPI PINs.

3. Media & Account Protection

All API endpoints, paid video streams, PDFs, mock tests, and admin tools enforce authentication and role-based authorization. Recorded streams and PDFs are served using short-lived signed URLs.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Students may request account deletion anytime from the Student App profile or via Support. Financial and audit records are retained strictly as required under applicable Indian tax and accounting laws.
